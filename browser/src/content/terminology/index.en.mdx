## Key Metrological Terms

A glossary of terms used throughout the ISQ Browser, adapted from JCGM 200 (VIM) and ISO 80000-1.

### Base Quantity
One of the seven quantities (length, mass, time, electric current, thermodynamic temperature, amount of substance, luminous intensity) that are dimensionally independent.

### Derived Quantity
A quantity defined as a product of powers of base quantities (e.g., velocity = length ÷ time).

### Dimension
An expression of the dependence of a quantity on the base quantities, as a product of powers corresponding to the base quantities.

### Unit
A real scalar quantity, defined and adopted by convention, used as a standard for measurement of quantities of the same kind.

### Measurement Unit
A unit adopted or recommended for a particular quantity.

### Quantity
A property of a phenomenon, body, or substance, to which a magnitude can be assigned.

### System of Quantities
Set of quantities together with the relations among them.
