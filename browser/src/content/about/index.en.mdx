## The International System of Quantities (ISQ)

The ISO 80000 & IEC 80000 series defines the **International System of Quantities (ISQ)** — the foundational vocabulary of physical quantities, units of measurement, and mathematical notation used across all fields of science, engineering, and commerce.

## What This Standard Covers

The series is organized into independently published parts:

- **Part 1**: General rules and definitions
- **Part 2**: Mathematical signs and symbols
- **Parts 3–13**: Quantities and units organized by domain (space and time, mechanics, thermodynamics, electromagnetism, light, acoustics, physical chemistry, atomic physics, characteristic numbers, condensed matter, information science)

## Publishers

- Most parts are published by **ISO** (International Organization for Standardization), prepared by ISO/TC 12
- Parts 6 and 13 are published by **IEC** (International Electrotechnical Commission), prepared by IEC/TC 25
- The two committees work in close cooperation

## Relationship to the SI

The ISQ provides the system of quantities underlying the **International System of Units (SI)**, maintained by the BIPM. The ISQ defines what quantities exist and how they relate; the SI defines the units for measuring them.
