## Academic Background

The ISQ Browser draws on academic work in metrology, conceptual engineering, and knowledge representation:

### Key References

- **Pendrill, L. (2024)** — Pendrill's concept system hierarchy adapted as the four-level structure (Kind → Quantity → Dedicated Quantity → Entry).
- **Fleischman, E. (1960)** — Origin of the "Sachgrösse" (entity quantity) concept at Level 2.
- **JCGM 200 (VIM)** — International Vocabulary of Metrology, source of glossary terms.
- **ISO 80000-1** — General rules and definitions for the ISQ.

### Standards

- ISO 80000:2019 (Parts 1–12) — Quantities and units
- IEC 80000:2022 (Parts 6, 13) — Electromagnetism, Information science
