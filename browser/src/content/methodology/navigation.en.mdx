## Navigation Modes

The ISQ Browser offers five complementary ways to explore the dataset:

### 1. By Domain
Start with a domain (quantities or mathematical notation) and drill down by part.

### 2. By Part
Browse a specific part (e.g., Part 3: Space and Time) to see all entries for that topic.

### 3. By Kind of Quantity
Group quantities by their underlying dimension (length, mass, time, etc.).

### 4. By Unit
Find all quantities measured by a given unit (e.g., meter, second).

### 5. By Dimension
Browse the physical dimensions and see which quantities share them.
