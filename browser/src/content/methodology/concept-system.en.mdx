## Four Levels Across Three Axes

The ISQ Browser organizes every quantity along a four-level hierarchy across three parallel axes:

### Level 0: Kind of Quantity
The most abstract category — a grouping of quantities that can be compared, added, and subtracted because they share the same dimension.

### Level 1: Quantity
A concrete physical concept. "Length" (as opposed to "width" or "height") all measure the same kind — they can be compared and added.

### Level 2: Dedicated Quantity
A quantity bound to a specific kind of entity. "Length of rod" is not abstract length; it is length as applied to rods.

### Level 3: Entry
The specific measured instance, e.g., "Length of rod 3, measured 2024-03-15".

## Three Axes

- **Quantity axis** — what is being measured (kind → entry)
- **Entity axis** — what the quantity is about (kind of entity → named individual)
- **Measurement axis** — how the measurement is expressed (symbol → units → value)
